NEW TO JIU-JITSU?

Your first class
at Titan.

Never trained before? That is completely fine. Here is what to expect when you join us in Summerville.

01

Come as
you are.

You do not need jiu-jitsu or martial arts experience. Come in, meet the Titan family, and get comfortable.

02

Learn the
basics.

Beginners are introduced to the fundamentals and guided through class. You are not expected to know jiu-jitsu already.

03

See what
you think.

Experience jiu-jitsu, meet the community, and decide whether training at Titan feels right for you or your child.
