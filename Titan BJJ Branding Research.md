# Titan BJJ branding research

Research date: 2026-09-01

Sources:
- Website: https://www.titanjiujitsusummerville.com/
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/titan_jiujitsu_llc/
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/TitanJiujitsuLLC

## Brand snapshot

- Business name: Titan Jiujitsu LLC
- Positioning: a locally owned Summerville, South Carolina jiu-jitsu program for youth, teens, and adults.
- Core promise: build confidence, discipline, resilience, respect, sportsmanship, teamwork, camaraderie, and physical fitness.
- Strongest existing headline: “TRAINING THE BEST TO BECOME THE BEST”
- Primary CTA: “START YOUR FREE TRIAL”
- Supporting phrases: “Building confidence in local youth,” “The Best Version of Yourself,” “Truly Top-Notch,” “Small local business in your area.”
- Community language from Facebook: “Our mission is to build resilience and self-confidence through jiujitsu, emphasizing sportsmanship, teamwork, and camaraderie to foster a respectful and supportive community.”
- Differentiator: all instructors are South Carolina natives; the school emphasizes a safe, supportive environment.

## Audience and programs

- Tiny Titans: ages 5–7; Gi and No-Gi classes.
- Medium Titans: ages 8+; Gi and No-Gi classes.
- Towering Titans: teens/adults; Gi and No-Gi classes.
- Facebook also promotes a homeschool Friday pilot for ages 6–14, framed around physical education, confidence, discipline, practical skills, and social support.

## Brand vocabulary / SEO seed keywords

Titan Jiujitsu, Titan Jiu-Jitsu, Summerville jiu-jitsu, Summerville SC martial arts, jiu-jitsu academy Summerville, kids jiu-jitsu, youth jiu-jitsu, teen jiu-jitsu, adult jiu-jitsu, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, BJJ classes, Gi, No-Gi, free trial class, confidence, discipline, resilience, respect, sportsmanship, teamwork, camaraderie, physical fitness, safe training environment, local martial arts school, homeschool physical education. “Brazilian jiu-jitsu” and “self-defense” are useful expansion terms to validate with the owner before publishing.

## Visual direction observed

- Existing imagery is people-first: students training, instructors, and community moments.
- The “Titan” naming system is extended into class names, which is a useful organizing device for the redesign.
- Facebook’s recent promotional creative uses energetic, direct language and a community/family emphasis: “LEARN. GROW. BELONG.” and “YOUR FAMILY.”
- Use real student/instructor photography as the primary visual asset; pair it with bold athletic typography, high contrast, and clear CTA blocks.
- The current site appears Wix-based and has a sparse, sectioned one-page structure. A redesign should improve hierarchy, class schedule scanability, mobile CTA visibility, and trust signals.

## Asset collection

Website-observed image and style assets are in `branding-assets/`; the page also referenced fonts, but those font downloads failed and are not included. The source manifest is included there as `manifest.json`; its original temporary paths are retained for provenance, while downloaded files are stored locally using stable asset-ID filenames. Not all social media assets were downloadable through the public profile views; Instagram exposed only a limited signed-out state, while Facebook exposed profile/post imagery and text but not a clean bulk asset export.

## Suggested redesign content structure

1. Hero: location + promise + “Book Your Free Trial” CTA.
2. Quick proof bar: youth / teens / adults, Gi + No-Gi, locally owned, safe supportive coaching.
3. Programs: Tiny, Medium, and Towering Titans with age ranges and schedule cards.
4. Why Titan: confidence, discipline, resilience, community.
5. Instructor/team and lineage.
6. Photo-led community section and testimonials/reviews.
7. Location, contact, schedule, and persistent free-trial CTA.
