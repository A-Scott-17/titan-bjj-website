Privacy policy

Information you provide

When you request a free class, Titan Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu LLC collects the name, email address, phone number, and class interest or age range you submit. Please provide a parent or guardian’s contact details when inquiring about a minor.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, help select an appropriate class, and communicate about Titan programs. We do not sell personal information.

Our website form is delivered through a third-party form service. That provider processes the details you submit to send the inquiry to Titan. We may also use service providers needed to operate our website and communicate with you.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the contact information we hold by emailing Titanjiujitsullc@gmail.com. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Contact
